# Glossary: Japanese listing terms and the English to use

Use these renderings so every brochure reads the same. Where a term has legal
weight (zoning, tenure, restrictions), keep the Japanese in brackets after
the English the first time it appears, because the buyer will need it to look
the rule up or ask an agent.

## Names

- Romanise with Hepburn and macrons: Ogikubo, Chūō, Kichijōji, Minami-Ogikubo 1-chōme, Suginami-ku.
- Give a person's or company's name in Japanese in brackets the first time: Amtic Co., Ltd. (アムティック(株)).
- Station and line names follow the operator's English name where one exists (JR Chūō Line, Keiō Inokashira Line).
- Do not translate a brand or shop name; describe it once if it helps (OK Store, a discount supermarket chain).
